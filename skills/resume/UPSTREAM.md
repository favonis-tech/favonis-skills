# Upstream

The script, templates and instructions are written for Favonis. The writing guide adapts one MIT-licensed source and takes ideas from several other public resume skills; no code or template from them is included.

Adapted text:

- [StoneLL1/resume-builder](https://github.com/StoneLL1/resume-builder) at `1339991e2191590091acc5df0bfd8037d009d4ce`, MIT; see [licenses/resume-builder.txt](licenses/resume-builder.txt). references/writing.md restates its `Resume-Writing-Guide-LLM.md` in English and condensed form: the decision order, the rules against invented or inflated facts, bullet shapes by role, section and situation guidance, the failure patterns, the self-check and the critique severities. The fact states in `素材.md` follow its claim map.

Ideas only, rewritten in this package's own words:

- [wangyafu/resume-skills](https://github.com/wangyafu/resume-skills): questions with suggested answers, showing a first draft before every answer is in, bold only on key terms, a page filled exactly.
- [KevinYoung-Kw/vibe-resume-skill](https://github.com/KevinYoung-Kw/vibe-resume-skill): a fact bank kept apart from per-job versions, the covered / ask / do-not-claim lists for a JD, asking before deleting the user's facts, the readability floor and bottom-whitespace limit.
- [youngbeauty/GodSU](https://github.com/youngbeauty/GodSU): strongest signals in the top third, the old-way → change → gain story, verifiable anchors, highlighted gaps, the three-questions-deep interview check.
- [Claycui828/ASu-resume-skills](https://github.com/Claycui828/ASu-resume-skills) (MIT): the inflation patterns used in the self-audit and checking school labels against official sources.
- [Li-Baichuan-James/cv-skill](https://github.com/Li-Baichuan-James/cv-skill) (MIT): asking about blocking facts before drafting, and the photo trade-off.
- [Jichengyuuuuu/resume-builder-skill](https://github.com/Jichengyuuuuu/resume-builder-skill): skill groups by role, bold-label bullets, follow-up advice from a hiring manager's view.
- [career-ops-hq/career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops) (MIT): job titles unchanged across tailored versions, the JD archived next to the version, gaps told to the user rather than papered over.
- [SankaiAI/ats-optimized-resume-agent-skill](https://github.com/SankaiAI/ats-optimized-resume-agent-skill) (MIT): the pass that makes bullets read as written by a person.
