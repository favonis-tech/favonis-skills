# Writing resume content

Read this before drafting, rewriting, tailoring or critiquing a resume. A resume is evidence for one target role, not an autobiography: in a few seconds the reader decides whether it matches, whether there is proof, and whether the person is worth an interview.

When two goals conflict, decide in this order: truth > fit to the target > strength of evidence > the person's own contribution > impact > scannability > polished wording.

## Facts

- Use only what the user's materials state or the user confirmed. Never invent an employer, title, date, degree, project, tool, award, ranking, user count, revenue, accuracy or percentage.
- Never upgrade a fact: 参与 does not become 主导 or Owner, "used" does not become 熟练 or 精通, a team result does not become a personal one, planned work does not become shipped, a course project does not become a commercial system, a tutorial does not become original work.
- No number: ask for one (before and after, volume, time saved, users). If there is none, use a real substitute: shipped, adopted by a named team, accepted by the client, coverage or scale, a deliverable, a decision it changed, outside recognition. Never ask the user to estimate a figure, never take one from an example, and never blur a figure into 约 or 30%+ so it is harder to check; keep the user's figure and its baseline.
- Tie each number to the person's action: action → intermediate effect → result. When they only took part, write 参与 or 支持 and that the metric reached X in the period, not that they caused it.
- Superlatives (最, 首个, 第一, 唯一, Top) need a named comparison set the user can show.
- School labels such as 985, 211, 双一流 or a ranking appear only when true; check the school's site or the Ministry of Education list when unsure. State the degree, a joint programme and the awarding institution exactly.
- A job title stays exactly as held in every version. Tailoring changes emphasis, never titles, employers or dates.
- Leave out employer-confidential details (internal code names, unreleased figures, internal links) unless the user says they may be shown.
- Personal data: name, phone, email, city, target role, and GitHub or portfolio links when relevant. Leave out ID number, home address, marital status, height and weight. Add a photo, 政治面貌 or 籍贯 only when the target employer or region expects them (often 国企, 事业单位 and civil service); foreign companies and English resumes usually omit photo, age and marital status.

## Digging out facts

Weak resumes usually lack material, not wording. For each experience find: context and goal, the problem, the person's role and its boundary, key actions, methods and tools, judgments and trade-offs, results, scale, and something verifiable. Useful questions:

- 你本人负责到什么程度：独立完成、主要负责，还是团队里负责其中一块？是哪一块？
- 之前是怎么做的，哪里不行，你改成了什么？
- 有没有前后对比的数字：耗时、成本、规模、准确率、用户量、调用量？
- 有没有能验证的东西：上线地址、GitHub、报告、演示、被谁采用？
- 有没有被认可的记录：获奖、排名、评级、表彰、被引用或被采纳？

Ask with `user_actions`, three to six questions in one batch, each with short suggested replies so the user picks rather than recalls (for ownership: 独立完成 / 主要负责 / 团队协作中负责一部分). Ask blocking facts first (target role, identity, school, degree, employers, titles and dates), then high-value ones (ownership, numbers, artifacts), then minor ones. A "没有" or "跳过" ends that thread: record it and do not ask again.

Questions are not a gate. Once the target, the name, a contact and one experience are known, draft and show the first version with 【待补充：…】 placeholders; people remember more when they see the page.

## Choosing and ordering

- One target role per version. With no target, infer it from the strongest material and say so.
- Typical order: header, optional summary, then education and experience by relevance. Students and new graduates usually put education first unless they have strong internships; experienced candidates lead with work and keep education short at the end. Then projects, research, competitions or open source, campus roles, skills, honours and certificates.
- Inside a section, newest first; across sections, relevance first. Never fake a chronology.
- Keep, from strongest: relevant with results > relevant with clear actions > transferable > signs of long-term commitment > weakly related > filler. If removing an item would not weaken fit, credibility or distinctiveness, cut or compress it.
- Length: students and juniors one page; two pages for substantial relevant experience. Older or unrelated roles get one line (company, title, dates). Do not cut a core result to reach one page.
- The top third must show the target role, the most relevant experience, two or three of the strongest signals and any verifiable links. Courses, hobbies and generic certificates do not belong there.

## Bullets

Default shape: [context or problem] + [the person's responsibility or judgment] + [action or method] + [result, impact or artifact]. Keep the shortest chain that makes sense and never print STAR labels.

- Engineering: problem → solution → key mechanism or trade-off → metric or launch.
- Business, product, operations, data: situation → analysis → judgment → action → result.
- Research: question → method → personal contribution → finding or output.
- English resumes: "Accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z", only when X and Y are in the facts.

Rules:

- One main point per bullet, one or two lines, three at most. Do not chain four or five things with 、.
- In Chinese resumes, open the important bullets with a bold label of two to six characters and a colon: `**订单查询提速**：…`.
- Ownership verbs, weakest first: 协助/支持 < 参与 < 负责 < 独立负责 < 主导 < Owner. Use the one the facts support. Engineering verbs: 设计, 实现, 搭建, 重构, 优化, 部署, 排查, 验证, 评估. Business verbs: 拆解, 分析, 识别, 定义, 制定, 推动, 协调, 复盘, 落地.
- An evolution story beats a feature list: what was wrong with the old way → what they changed → what it gained.
- Go as deep in technical detail (parameters, scale, thresholds) as the user can defend. Every term on the page must be one they can explain in an interview.
- Bold only what a reader should catch while skimming: a project name, a core technique, a key result. One to three per bullet, never a whole sentence or a plain verb.

## Sections

- **Header:** name, phone, email, city, target role as the headline, and GitHub or portfolio when relevant. Write a link as its visible address (github.com/name) so it still works on paper.
- **Education:** school, degree and major, dates; GPA or rank only when favourable; three to five courses only for students with thin experience and courses close to the role; honours.
- **Summary (自我评价):** optional. Keep it only when two to four lines of facts place the candidate: identity, years and field + two or three core strengths + one representative result. Never 学习能力强, 吃苦耐劳, 责任心强 or similar claims without evidence.
- **Work and internships:** company, title, dates, team or city; an optional line of context; two to four strong bullets, up to five or six for the most relevant role.
- **Projects:** why it was done, the problem, the person's part, why that approach, the hard part or trade-off, the result, the artifact. One to three relevant projects. A tutorial copy is not original work; label course work as such.
- **Research:** question → method → contribution → output; for a job, only what serves the role.
- **Campus roles:** for students without relevant internships and for 国企 or general-track roles; still scope → action → scale or result.
- **Skills:** a keyword index backed by the experience above. Group by the dimensions the role cares about, rank the strongest first, and use 了解, 熟悉, 熟练 and 精通 honestly. Drop basics such as Office unless the role asks for them.

| Role | Skill groups |
| --- | --- |
| Backend | languages, frameworks and middleware, databases, distributed systems and concurrency, DevOps and cloud |
| Algorithms, AI | models and training, data and evaluation, inference and deployment, frameworks |
| Product | product methods, data skills, domain knowledge, tools |
| Operations, growth | growth methods, content, data analysis, channels and platforms, tools |
| Data analysis | SQL and Python, BI tools, statistics and experiments, business understanding |
| Design | design skills, tools, portfolio |
| Marketing | brand, content, paid channels, attribution |
| Finance | accounting and finance, modelling and valuation, data tools, industry |
| Sales, BD | methods, customer types, largest deal size, industry network |

## By situation

- **Engineering:** real problems, engineering choices, mechanisms, performance, stability, scale, launches. Production beats demos; a list of frameworks or CRUD pages proves little.
- **AI, LLM, agents:** why an agent or retrieval was needed, inputs and outputs, the modules, how the model and tools are called, the fallback, how it was evaluated and iterated, and the person's part. For fine-tuning: data sources and cleaning, why that training setup, evaluation and error analysis. "Did LoRA fine-tuning on platform X" is not a highlight.
- **Data analysis:** business question → metric definitions → method (cohorts, funnels, attribution, experiments) → judgment → action → result, not 取数 and 写日报.
- **Product and operations:** user or business problem → analysis → strategy or product action → coordination → data result. AI products also show model limits, prompt or workflow design, evaluation and the feedback loop.
- **国企, 央企, 事业单位:** requirement intake, delivery, cross-team coordination, acceptance, documentation and team organisation; less specialist jargon; campus roles carry more weight.
- **Students without internships:** course projects, 大创, research, competitions, open source, demos, content creation, communities and campus organisations can prove real commitment, independent delivery and a method. Several small real signals add up; do not dress them up as big ones.
- **Career changers:** old experience → transferable abilities in the target role's language → new projects or work as proof. Wanting to switch is not evidence.
- **Weaker schools:** state education accurately and briefly; lead with strong role evidence.
- **考研复试, 保研, academic:** grades and fundamentals, English, specific contributions to research or competitions, research interests. Label coursework papers as coursework.

## Tailoring to a job description

1. Save the JD next to the version, for example `简历/JD-公司-岗位.md`, so later edits can be checked against it.
2. Split it into core tasks, hard skills and tools, domain, soft skills and keywords.
3. Map each requirement to evidence and show the user three lists: **covered** (already evidenced; reuse the JD's wording where it truly describes the work), **ask** (they might have it; one short question each), **do not claim** (no evidence; leave it out and mention it for the interview or cover letter). Give no match score.
4. Copy the base source to a new file for this target. Reorder sections, bullets and skills toward the JD and rewrite the headline and summary; titles, employers and dates stay. The fact bank stays unchanged.

## Keeping the user's content

When revising the user's own resume, keep every company, role, project, date, metric, link and fact they wrote unless they approve its removal. Shortening wording without losing a fact is fine. Deleting an entry or bullet, merging entries so a fact disappears, dropping a metric, or swapping a specific line for a generic one needs approval: propose a named list of cuts. 全部保留 or 不要删 outranks the page target; use two pages or a denser layout instead.

## Language

- Plain words. 赋能, 抓手, 闭环, 聚焦, 深度参与, 全面提升 and 有效促进 stay only when a concrete object, action and result follow.
- Make it sound like a person: not every bullet in the same mould, not all 通过……实现……, no intern written like an executive, no more concepts than facts, varied openings.
- No 我 in bullets. Keep punctuation (full-width in Chinese text), dates (`2024.07 – 2025.06`), number formats and units consistent.
- Keep the user's language; technical terms may stay in English.

## Self-audit before delivering

For every claim, picture the interviewer three questions deep: 这个 40% 怎么测的？基线是什么？你具体写了哪部分？ If the user could not defend it, soften or drop it, and tell them which lines and why; that is interview preparation too.

Check the usual inflation patterns: role inflation (参与 → Owner), a whole-system story for one module, a famous company or star count carrying the claim, team results shown as personal, precise-looking numbers without denominator, baseline or time window, a switched denominator, superlatives without a comparison set, planned work written as shipped, a partner school written as one's own degree.

Last check: one target; two or three strong signals in the top third; core experience first; the person's own role visible in every entry; results, scale or artifacts present; numbers tied to actions; skills backed by experience; nothing invented or upgraded; nothing irrelevant; every 【】 resolved.

## Critiques

When asked to review (帮我看看, 锐评), report by severity: P0 for truth, positioning or problems that fail screening; P1 for weak fit, evidence or results; P2 for language, order and layout. For each: the problem → why it hurts screening → how to fix it → a rewritten example. Do not rebuild the resume unless asked.

After delivering a resume, you may add three to five specific suggestions from the view of a hiring manager one or two levels above the target role: gaps worth filling, questions the interview is likely to probe, risks. Each must be about this resume; no generic advice.
